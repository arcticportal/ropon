Systems and related organizations that coordinate or track observing activities & infrastructure in the polar regions.

This registry has been released as a phase 1 version for demonstration purposes. Plans are underway for improvements in scope, functionality, and interoperability. Feedback is appreciated.
RoPON was created as a collaborative effort by the Sustaining Arctic Observing Networks initiative (SAON), the Arctic Portal, and the SAON Polar Observing Assets Working Group (POAwg), and is funded by the NOAA GOMO Arctic Research Program.
